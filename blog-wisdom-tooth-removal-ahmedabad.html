Do You Need Your Wisdom Tooth Removed?

X-ray showing impacted wisdom tooth — Dr. Siddharth's Dental Care, Ahmedabad

Wisdom teeth (third molars) usually appear between ages 17–25. In many patients in Ahmedabad, there simply isn't enough room in the jaw — leading to impaction, pain, and infection risk.

Signs your wisdom tooth needs attention

  • Pain or swelling at the back of the jaw
  • Difficulty opening your mouth fully
  • Bad taste or smell from the back of the mouth
  • Swollen, tender gums around the back molar
  • Headaches or jaw stiffness

The procedure

Performed under local anaesthesia (sometimes with sedation for complex cases). A small incision may be needed for fully impacted teeth. The procedure typically takes 20–45 minutes per tooth.

Modern surgical equipment for wisdom tooth extraction — Ahmedabad clinic

Day-by-day recovery timeline

  • Day 1: Swelling begins, bite on gauze, rest with head elevated
  • Day 2–3: Peak swelling and discomfort — ice packs help most here
  • Day 4–5: Swelling starts reducing, soft foods only
  • Day 7: Stitches removed if needed; most discomfort gone
  • Day 10–14: Fully healed for most patients

Cost in Ahmedabad

Wisdom tooth removal costs ₹3,000–₹8,000 per tooth depending on impaction severity and whether it's fully or partially erupted.
